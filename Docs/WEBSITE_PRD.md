## 2. Product Vision

Gatekeeper QA Website will be a professional online platform that helps SaaS startups understand Gatekeeper QA's services, build trust in the company, and initiate a conversation about their software quality needs.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Early-stage software companies often move quickly without having a dedicated QA function. This can result in:

- Bugs reaching customers
- Insufficient testing processes
- Low release confidence
- Requirements being misunderstood
- Regression issues
- Limited visibility into product quality

Gatekeeper QA needs a professional website that communicates how it helps these companies reduce software quality risks.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Primary User — Startup Founder

The founder wants to:

- Understand Gatekeeper QA's services
- Determine whether QA support is needed
- Understand the potential value and ROI
- Contact Gatekeeper QA

### Secondary User — CTO / Engineering Lead

The technical leader wants to:

- Understand Gatekeeper QA's testing capabilities
- Understand the QA process
- Verify technical competence
- Determine whether Gatekeeper QA can integrate with their workflow

---

## 5. Business Goals

The website must:

- Build trust and credibility
- Generate qualified leads
- Explain Gatekeeper QA's services
- Demonstrate QA expertise
- Showcase case studies and testing evidence
- Support future client acquisition

## 6. User Goals
