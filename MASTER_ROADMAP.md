# Target Customers

## Primary Market

SaaS startups building web and cloud applications.

## Ideal Customer Profile

- Early-stage startups
- 5–50 employees
- Preparing launch or scaling products
- Need external QA expertise

## Problems We Solve

- Bugs reaching users
- Lack of testing process
- Limited QA resources
- Low release confidence

## Positioning

Gatekeeper QA helps SaaS startups build confidence in their software through professional testing, automation, and quality engineering support.
